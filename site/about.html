About AT Consulting LLC

Decades of combined experience in athletic training education and CAATE accreditation review.

Our Story

AT Consulting LLC is led by a doctorally-prepared (EdD) athletic training educator and experienced program leader, credentialed as an LAT, ATC, and CSCS. With over 10 years of experience as a CAATE peer reviewer, our founder brings firsthand insight into both sides of the accreditation process.

10+ Years as a Peer Reviewer

Extensive experience evaluating self-study reports and conducting site visits as a CAATE peer reviewer, giving us an insider's view of what reviewers look for.

Program Leadership Experience

Years of experience as an athletic training educator and program leader, with direct knowledge of what it takes to run a successful, accredited program.

Specialized Expertise

Deep expertise in professional athletic training education, clinical education, assessment, evidence-based practice, and accreditation-related program development.
