Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about CAATE accreditation and working with us.

What is CAATE accreditation

What is CAATE accreditation?

CAATE (Commission on Accreditation of Athletic Training Education) is the body that accredits athletic training education programs in the U.S. Accreditation confirms a program meets established professional standards.

Why does CAATE accreditation matter?

It's generally required for graduates to sit for the BOC certification exam and practice as athletic trainers, and it signals program quality to students, employers, and institutions.

What's involved in a CAATE Comprehensive Program Review?

Programs submit a self-study report addressing CAATE's standards, followed by an onsite (or virtual) review team visit, interviews, and a final accreditation decision.

Our services & scope

Is AT Consulting LLC affiliated with CAATE?

No. We are an independent consulting firm. We do not represent CAATE, review on its behalf, or have authority over accreditation decisions.

Does hiring AT Consulting LLC guarantee accreditation?

No. We help you prepare strong documentation and practices, but accreditation decisions are made solely by CAATE.

How is a mock review different from CAATE's official review?

Our mock reviews use the same standards and a similar format to CAATE's process, but feedback is developmental and confidential to your program — it carries no official weight with CAATE.

Process & timeline

What does working with you typically look like?

It usually starts with a free consultation to understand your program's needs, then we scope one of our packages (or a custom combination) and set a timeline around your self-study or review deadlines.

How long does preparation typically take?

It varies by program size and where you are in the cycle — some engagements are a few weeks of document review, others span several months alongside a full self-study cycle.

Do you work remotely, onsite, or both?

Both. Self-study reviews and workshops are typically virtual; mock site visits are conducted onsite (travel expenses apply).
