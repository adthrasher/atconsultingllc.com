Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

AT Consulting LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates atconsultingllc.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to browse this Site. We collect information in the following limited ways:

  • Information you send us directly. If you email us (for example, at info@atconsultingllc.com), we receive whatever information you include in that message, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your inquiry. We use this information only to respond to you and to provide the services you request.
  • Aggregate site analytics. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand overall traffic to the Site. GoatCounter does not use cookies, does not collect personally identifying information, and does not track visitors across other websites. It reports aggregate, non-identifying data such as page views, referring sites, approximate location (country/region), and browser or device type.

Cookies

This Site does not set cookies of its own, and our analytics provider does not use cookies.

How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to respond to inquiries, operate and improve the Site, and understand general traffic patterns. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This Site links to third-party destinations, such as a mailto: link to our email address. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party sites or services you may access through links on this Site.

Data Retention

We retain email correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry or to comply with our legal and business recordkeeping obligations.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for institutional and professional audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
