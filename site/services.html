Our Services

End-to-end support for every stage of the CAATE accreditation process.

Independent Accreditation-Readiness Consulting

We provide independent, outside consulting to support athletic training programs preparing for their CAATE Comprehensive Program Review — helping identify strengths, assess the sufficiency of existing documentation, flag areas that need additional attention, and build a focused plan for final preparation.

Through this process, we provide an independent assessment of your program's documented evidence of compliance and identify areas that may warrant additional attention prior to the comprehensive programmatic review.

What We Offer

Self-Study Report Review

Line-by-line review of your self-study report against current CAATE standards, with detailed written feedback.

Mock Site Visits

A full-day simulated site visit, including faculty and student interviews, to prepare your program for the real thing.

Curriculum Mapping

Map your existing curriculum to CAATE standards and identify gaps before your official review.

Standards Update Briefings

Stay current with CAATE standards changes through periodic briefings tailored to your program.

Clinical Education Site Audits

Evaluate your clinical education sites and preceptor agreements for compliance with CAATE requirements.

Post-Review Action Planning

If your program receives citations, we help you build and execute a clear corrective action plan.
